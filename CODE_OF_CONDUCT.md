# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Physics Tracker is intended to be a respectful, evidence-oriented project for contributors and
users with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Assume good faith while asking for clarification.
- Keep discussions relevant to the repository.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's study data, credentials, database, or private
  correspondence.
- Give credit for sourced scientific or technical material.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive or bad-faith participation.
- Sexualized or violent content.
- Encouraging others to violate privacy, security, or licensing requirements.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when behavior
violates this policy. Reports should be made privately through GitHub's reporting tools or the
maintainer's profile contact options. Do not include sensitive data in a public report.

This is a project-specific code of conduct, not a claim of affiliation with another organization.
